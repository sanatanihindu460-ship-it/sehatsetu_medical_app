THE CHALLENGE

From a long journey to a connected care journey.

Rural patients may face specialist shortages, long travel, referral delays, poor connectivity and fragmented medical information.

ONE PLATFORM

More than just a video call.

We connect the complete healthcare journey.

🩺

Online Consultation

Connect patients and frontline health workers with doctors and specialists remotely.

🧠

Smart Triage

Assist health workers in identifying urgent cases and routing patients to appropriate care.

📋

Digital Health Record

Keep consultations, prescriptions, reports and follow-ups together for continuity of care.

🔄

Referral Tracking

Track a referral from the local facility to the district hospital and back to follow-up.

📶

Low Connectivity

Support audio, text and store-and-forward workflows when internet connectivity is weak.

💊

Medicine Visibility

Show medicine availability at participating facilities and help reduce unnecessary travel.

HOW IT WORKS

One patient. One connected journey.

01

Patient visits local health worker

Symptoms and basic vitals are recorded digitally.

02

Digital triage

The system assists in identifying risk and the appropriate level of care.

03

Doctor consultation

Patient connects through video, audio or low-bandwidth communication.

04

Prescription or referral

The doctor records the care plan and creates a referral when required.

05

Follow-up

Follow-up reminders and referral completion keep the care journey connected.
